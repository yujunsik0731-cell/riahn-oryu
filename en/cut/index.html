Home/Haircuts

A great cut is decided
before the scissors move

The same length can look completely different depending on head shape and hair flow. We analyze both — plus your face shape — so the cut falls into place at home, even if you're no good with a blow-dryer.

Men's cuts include styling, and if your sides puff out we recommend adding a side down perm. Want volume or waves too? See our perms & straightening guide.

Recommended for

  • Anyone with no time for daily styling
  • Men whose sides puff out soon after a cut
  • Layered cuts that flatter your face shape
  • Students near Oryu-dong looking for a student cut

FAQ

Haircut FAQ

Do you cut students' and children's hair?
Yes — student cuts are ₩18,000, and we also do side down-cuts for boys. For young children, please contact the salon.
Can I visit without a reservation?
Walk-ins are welcome, but you may need to wait. Booking through Naver — or simply calling us — guarantees your preferred time.
How long do services take?
Cuts 30–40 min, color 60–90 min, regular perms 90–120 min, and heat perms or straightening 150–210 min, depending on length and condition.
