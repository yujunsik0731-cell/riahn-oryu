Home/FAQ
Can I book without a Naver account?
Yes. You can book through Instagram DM at @riahnhair_oryu. Send us your preferred date, time and service, and we will confirm by reply. Phone booking is also available at +82-2-2685-1141. Foreign guests are welcome — we respond in English, Japanese and Chinese with translation support.
Can I visit without a reservation?
Walk-ins are welcome, but you may need to wait. Booking through Naver — or simply calling us — guarantees your preferred time.
Which designer should I choose for my first visit?
For men's cuts, down perms and volume perms, choose Jiwon; for layered cuts, C-curl perms and volume magic, Soyoung; for restorative straightening, texture repair and gray coverage, director Hyelim Han. You can also select "designer recommendation" when booking and we'll match you.
Do prices differ by designer?
Menu prices are the base rate. The final price can vary with the designer's seniority and your hair's length and condition — we confirm the exact price during your consultation, before we start.
Are any discounts running?
Perms and color are 30% off when you book designer Jiwon by name. Student cuts are ₩18,000 (student ID required). Discounts cannot be combined.
My hair is badly damaged — can I still get magic straightening?
We diagnose your hair first. If the damage is severe, we combine Nanoheal treatment or start with restorative straightening to repair the texture — we never push a service your hair can't handle.
How long do services take?
Cuts 30–40 min, color 60–90 min, regular perms 90–120 min, and heat perms or straightening 150–210 min, depending on length and condition.
Is parking available?
Building parking is limited. Please use the Oryu Art Hall (Oryu Culture Center) lot nearby — we cover the parking fee; just let our front desk know.
How often should I color my gray roots?
Every 4–6 weeks works best. Root touch-ups (from ₩68,000) keep the color natural while minimizing damage.
How should I care for my hair after a perm or color?
Avoid shampooing for 48 hours and use perm/color-safe shampoo and treatment. Your designer will walk you through a home-care routine suited to your hair.
Do you cut students' and children's hair?
Yes — student cuts are ₩18,000, and we also do side down-cuts for boys. For young children, please contact the salon.
