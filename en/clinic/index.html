Hair Clinic & Scalp Care

Hair and scalp tired from repeated treatments? Health comes back before style does.

Home/Clinic

The base of every good style is
healthy hair and scalp

Even the best perm or color won't last on damaged hair. Our Nanoheal clinic works in three steps by damage level — from basic texture repair to recovery for severely over-processed hair. Once the texture is back, plan your next look with our perm & straightening guide.

The head spa starts with a scalp diagnosis, then deep cleansing and a relaxing massage — a quiet break in Oryu-dong on a tiring day.

Recommended for

  • Rough, brittle texture from repeated perms and color — Nanoheal 2–3
  • Preventing damage alongside a perm or color — Nanoheal 1
  • Oily, itchy or odor-prone scalp — head spa
  • High stress and in need of deep relaxation — 40-min head spa

FAQ

Clinic FAQ

My hair is badly damaged — can I still get magic straightening?
We diagnose your hair first. If the damage is severe, we combine Nanoheal treatment or start with restorative straightening to repair the texture — we never push a service your hair can't handle.
How should I care for my hair after a perm or color?
Avoid shampooing for 48 hours and use perm/color-safe shampoo and treatment. Your designer will walk you through a home-care routine suited to your hair.
